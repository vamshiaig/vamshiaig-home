Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

The short version: vamshiaig.com is a static, informational site — it sets no tracking cookies and collects no personal data about you. The only personal data VAMSHIAIG LLP ever holds is what you choose to give us when you sign in to, or purchase from, one of our products (such as Vibe Resume). We never sell or rent your data, and we never store your card details.

Who we are

In this policy “we”, “us” and “our” mean VAMSHIAIG LLP. You can reach us at contact@vamshiaig.com for any privacy question.

This website (vamshiaig.com)

The corporate site you are reading is a single static page. It has no sign-in, no forms, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking scripts. It does not set cookies and does not store anything in your browser. Whether it appears in light or dark mode is decided by your own device setting — we don't record that choice.

Our products

Personal data is only ever involved when you actively use one of our products. Each product describes its own data handling in detail on its own site — for example, see the Vibe Resume privacy policy. Across our products, the categories of data we may process are:

  • Account email — only if you sign in. We use it to send a one-tap magic link and to associate your account with anything you save (such as a reserved viberesume.in/{handle} slug). No password is stored.
  • Product content you create — several of our tools run entirely in your browser, so the files you open (for example, a resume PDF) are processed on your device and are never uploaded to or stored on our servers. Where a feature is explicitly opt-in AI, only the short text you type into that form is sent to our AI provider for the rewrite — never your uploaded file — and it is not used to train their models.
  • Usage records — when you use a metered or paid feature we log a small row (your user id, which feature, and when) to enforce quotas and understand what the service costs us. These records note the size of a request, never its content.
  • Payment records — if you buy a paid pack, the transaction is handled by our payment gateway (Razorpay). We receive a confirmation and a payment reference so we can grant what you bought and support you; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Anonymous product analytics — inside our products (not on this corporate site) we may use privacy-respecting analytics to see which features are used, in order to improve them. This is not tied to your product content.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We don't store your payment card details — our payment gateway handles them.
  • We don't upload or read the files you process locally in a browser-based tool.
  • We don't require an account to use the free, default path of our tools.

Your choices

You can use the free paths of our products without giving us an email at all. If you have an account, you can ask us to access or delete your data — including your email and anything saved against your account — by emailing contact@vamshiaig.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as our products evolve; the date above reflects the latest version.
